Help

Learn NeBuNotes

01What it is for

NeBuNotes is a songwriting and arrangement notebook that runs entirely in your browser. It keeps lyrics, chords and song structure in one place, so a song can move from a rough idea to something a band can rehearse from.

Write itLyrics, chords, notes
Arrange itSections, bars, tempo
Post itPrint, Word, PDF, share

There is no account and no server. Your songs are stored in this browser, and you decide what to export.

02Quick start

  1. Name the song. Fill in the title, tempo, time signature and key at the top.
  2. Add sections. Use Add section to create an Intro, Verse, Chorus and so on. Each type gets its own colour.
  3. Write lyric lines. Type or paste lyrics into a section. Press Add lyric line for each new line.
  4. Add chords. Open Chords on a line, add a chord and choose the word it sits above.
  5. Check the result. Press the Preview button next to the logo to see the song as it will be shared.

03Sections and bars

Every section has a length in bars. The numbers run through the whole song, so the timeline, the Song contents and the Drummer View always agree.

Verse 1, 8 bars12345678
Two lyric linesBars 1-4Bars 5-8

Bars are shared evenly between the lyric lines in a section. With more bars than lines, each line covers a range such as Bars 5-6. With as many lines as bars, each line is a single bar. Drag the grip beside a line, or a section in the timeline, to reorder it.

04Chords

A chord is attached to a word, so it stays in the right place when the lyric changes. In the Preview and in exports, the chord is printed above that word.

Am C
Walking in the rain
  • Choose a chord from the list or type your own, for example Asus2 or F#m7b5.
  • Open Chord Maker in the Tools menu to build a chord from frets and copy its name.
  • Typing inline also works when importing text: [Am]Walking in the [C]rain.

05Views

EditorWhere you write and arrange.
PreviewThe finished song sheet. The button beside the logo toggles it.
Drummer ViewStructure, bar numbers, tempo and drummer notes, for rehearsal.
Chord LegendOnly the chord diagrams for this song. The guitar button beside Preview toggles it.
Focus modeView menu. Removes boxes and headings so only the song remains.
Dynamic timelineView menu. Fits the arrangement timeline to the width available; switch it off to scroll instead. Bar numbers hide when the boxes get too small.

The View menu also holds layout (automatic, mobile or desktop) and the size control, which scales the whole interface from 80% to 200%.

06Saving and sharing

Changes are saved automatically in this browser, and the indicator in the top bar confirms it. File, then Songs keeps a library so you can switch between songs, duplicate them or delete them.

ActionUse it for
Export project (.json)A full backup of one song, including everything the app stores.
Export Word / PDFA song sheet in the same layout. Choose the full song, a lead sheet (chords only) or lyrics only, with optional notes and drummer notes.
Import Word (.docx)Bring a song sheet from Word or Google Docs back in.
Songs: Back up allEvery saved song in one file, to move to another device.

Browser storage can be cleared by the browser or by you. Export a backup now and then to keep your songs safe.

07Writing aids

View, then Syllables and rhymes shows a count beside each lyric line and a matching letter for lines that rhyme within a section. The counts are approximate and work best for English and Turkish.
